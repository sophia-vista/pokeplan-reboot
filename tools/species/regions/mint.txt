# The Mint Region (Region.MINT, the finance module's Ledger Research): steady, careful Pokémon for saving, counting and keeping
# things safe: Steel, Rock and Ground (mines, vaults, coins and gems), fossils and relics (old records), hoarders and keepers.
# Starts with the Sinnoh starters (Piplup is too proud to take a handout). Patrons: Diancie (the jewel) and Melmetal, last in
# the list. Super Rare lines: Beldum (a computer of four brains) and Duraludon (a steel bridge that holds).
# Not released until the module ships.
#
# In the order of a mainline regional dex: the starters first, then the lines by rarity (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Super Rare) in
# National Dex order with every stage next to its pre-evolution, then the quest and milestone species (ultra beasts, paradox,
# legendary, mythical) and the patrons last. A line is never split: a species is in at most one module region (the other lists
# in this folder; the generator checks it), and every evolution is in the same list as the Pokémon it evolves from.
# One species per line, by English name or PokeAPI identifier; blank lines and # lines are ignored.
# Legendary, mythical, Ultra Beast and Paradox species stay in the dex but are quest and milestone rewards, never egg drops.
# Curation: docs/region-curation-prompt.md. After editing:
#   python3 -I tools/species/generate.py
#   ./gradlew :pokemon-core:jvmTest
