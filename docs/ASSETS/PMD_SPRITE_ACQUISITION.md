## Method and definitions

* Search stages are kept apart: *source searched*, *source found*, *file located*, *file downloaded*, *file visually inspected*,
  *animation metadata validated*, *permission verified*, *integrated*. `PMD_SPRITE_SOURCE_CATALOG.md` says how far each source got.
* Animations are read from each folder's `AnimData.xml` with `CopyOf` aliases resolved (for example `Strike` is `Attack`), and a
  sheet must exist for the resolved name.
* **COMPLETE_PMD** here means Idle, Walk, Attack, Hurt and Sleep all exist. Faint is reported but not required: SpriteCollab does
  not define a Faint animation for these species (only Miraidon's Low Power form has one). SpriteCollab's own "minimal complete"
  set also asks for Swing and Double; both are listed in the manifest per folder.
* **PARTIAL_PMD**: genuine PMD art with fewer of those animations (Idle at least). **PMD_STATIC**: PMD-style art without
  animation (none found). **ALTERNATIVE_STYLE**: other overworld or battle sprites. **PORTRAIT_ONLY**: a portrait, no sprite.
  **PERMISSION_PENDING**: art exists, reuse permission not established. **MISSING**: nothing.
* The class is that of the species' default form. A species drawn only as other forms (Pyroar female, Falinks Brass and Trooper,
  Gimmighoul Roaming, Miraidon Low Power) is PARTIAL_PMD with the forms listed as separate records in the manifest.

## Sources

Every source searched, its licence and result: `PMD_SPRITE_SOURCE_CATALOG.md`. Access limits: DeviantArt downloads need a login; TikTok videos cannot be downloaded; Discord archives (SpriteCollab's submission channel) are not reachable from the sandbox; GitHub's web UI and API are blocked (git over the proxy works, so trees and blobs are read with `git ls-tree` and `git show` in blobless clones).

## Integrated sheets and attribution

SpriteCollab sprites are CC BY-NC 4.0 (non-commercial use with credit), like the rest of `assets/pmd`; every form below has its
artists in `assets/pmd/credits.tsv`, which the Pokédex shows. The sheets were produced by `tools/sprites/fetch_pmd_new.py` at
SpriteCollab commit `709f2e32ae4b21d11628883fd2356bb2d5f75244` through the existing pipeline: the front-facing row of Idle (Walk if
there is no Idle), Sleep, Eat, Hop, and three Walk directions, cut to one box per row, with the frame timings from `AnimData.xml`
and the shiny coat when it has the same frames. `CopyOf` aliases are resolved when the rows are cut (Strike to Attack and so on).
The original `AnimData.xml` and sheet files stay upstream; their SHA-256 hashes (AnimData) and our sheets' hashes are in the manifest.

## 64-species status

Per species: class, animations, source, local file and artists are in `PMD_SPRITE_MANIFEST.json` (regenerate with `tools/sprites/pmd_audit.py`); the open ones are in `PMD_SPRITE_GAPS.md`.

## Not integrated, and why

* **Pyroar (female only), Falinks (Brass and Trooper only)**: upstream has no regular coat, and the app's forms (`pyroar-male`,
  `falinks`) would show the wrong Pokémon.
* **Gimmighoul**: only Roaming is drawn (the app's `gimmighoul-roaming`, form 10263, already had it from the older pin); the Chest form is not.
* **Miraidon**: only Low Power Mode is drawn; it is integrated as `miraidon-low-power-mode` (10268), not as the default form.
* **Ting-Lu**: the root folder (Idle only) is integrated; the `Temp` folder (0001) is a full set under a "Temp" form name that has no
  app form.
* **The eight permission-pending species**: see `PMD_SPRITE_GAPS.md`.

## Recommendations

1. Keep this report's pin (`709f2e3`) for the 23 forms; when SpriteCollab's tracker catches up, re-pin `fetch_pmd.py` and run it.
2. Ask the artists for the seven fork-only sheets (Pokejavi., JFain and the unnamed contributors) or wait for SpriteCollab to approve
   them; ask the Auto Chess maintainers who drew their Okidogi.
3. Decide separately whether DarkusShadow's overworld sheets (free to use with credit) are worth a deliberate fallback renderer for
   the nine Paldea species that have nothing else; they are not PMD style and must not enter the PMD renderer.
