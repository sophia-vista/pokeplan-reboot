# Economy simulation

Generated by `./gradlew :plan-core:simulateEconomy`. Do not edit by hand.

Plays 180 days for 60 simulated players per profile through the real reward engine: sessions hatch eggs (greedy region choice, 30% Deep Focus), tasks pay candies, drops and Egg meter points, duplicates are transferred, Rare Candies go to the buddy. Medians, with the 10th to 90th percentile in brackets.

| Profile | Days a week | Tasks a day | Sessions a day |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light | 5.0 | 1-3 | 0-1 |
| Typical | 6.0 | 3-7 | 1-3 |
| Heavy | 6.6 | 6-12 | 2-5 |

## Targets

- Basic Plan dex (57 hatchable species) in about 60 days for a typical player.
- Ranks quick at first, slower after: Apprentice (Kanto) within about 2 days and Ace within about 2 weeks for a typical player; each rank takes longer than the one before.
- Evolving everything in the Plan Region once needs 83 evolutions: 70 Super Candies, 3808 to 7170 Rare Candies (with and without the catch-up discount), 12 stones, 1 Link Cords, 3 trials.
