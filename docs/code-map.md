# Code map

Where everything lives and what each module's storage looks like. `CLAUDE.md` holds the short version and the rules; this file holds
the detail that used to sit in its Layout section (moved here on 11 November 2026 so it is not loaded into every session).
**Grep this file for the module you are about to touch; do not read it whole.** Update it in the same change as the code it describes.

- `pokemon-core/`: the Pokémon side, reusable by every app in the planned family (see "Platform and apps" in docs/design.md); Kotlin Multiplatform (JVM and wasmJs), no UI, knows nothing about tasks or focus. Packages under `app.pokeplan.core`: `dex` (the National Dex: species, forms, evolutions, regional dexes, Pokédex text, types, rarity, egg rarity tables and hatch rules; the Dex tables are the generated JSON files `src/commonMain/resources/dex/*.json`, read by `DexParser`; the JVM and Android read them as resources and the web build gets them built into its code by `generateDexWebSources`), `reward` (the reward engine, candy reward tables, trainer rank), and `pokemon` (an owned Pokémon with IVs, EVs, nature, ability, gender, shiny, friendship; stat formula; hatching; items, bag, perks and transfer; candy, friendship and shiny rules; evolution rules; and the Guardian role of legendaries and mythicals in `Perks.kt`). `jvmMain/tools` holds JVM-only reports such as `./gradlew :pokemon-core:curationReport`.
- **Package layout (0.195.1, owner's modular rule):** every module's code sits in its own package in each layer. `composeApp/.../ui/`: `plan/`, `care/`, `heal/`, `mint/`, `base/`, `link/`, `world/`, `gear/` (screens and logic), `pokemon/` (the Pokémon tab, PC, Pokédex, Bag, Mart, party and perks), `launcher/`, `theme/`, and `screens/` for what every module shares (Home, Me, Settings, Menu, Components, Modules, onboarding, tutorials). `app-core/.../app/`: `base/`, `care/`, `heal/` (with the Huawei Health parsing), `link/`, `mint/`, `world/` (alerts and `WorldActions.kt`), `gear/` (`GearActions.kt`: helpers, timers), `plan/` (nudges, task links), `reminders/`, `storage/`, `quest/`; the package root keeps `GameStore`, `AppData` and what several modules share. World and Gear actions are extension functions on `GameStore` (import them from their package). `androidApp/`: `platform/` (sensors, torch, haptics, files, phone state), `health/`, `reminders/`, `widgets/`, `launcher/`, `huawei/`; the classes the manifest names stay in the root package.

- `care-core/`: the Care module's rules (routines, calm breaks, mood check-ins, prompts, trends; package `app.pokeplan.care`), JVM and wasmJs; its screens are `care/Care.kt` (Modules → Care, `AppModel.care`, switched by `Settings.careModule`). It meets the game only through `CareEvent` (`RewardEngine.care`); `CareStore` in app-core keeps its own save and pays through the `World` interface (docs/pokelife/FEATURES/POKEMON_ECOSYSTEM.md).
- `heal-core/`: the Heal module's rules (docs/pokelife `MODULES/HEAL.md` section 22): manual activity, hydration, sleep, body measurements and optional goals, units and trends in words, and what may pay Field Research (`HealRules`); JVM and wasmJs, no UI, knows nothing about Pokémon or rewards (package `app.pokeplan.heal`). `HealStore` in app-core keeps its own save (`HealData`) and pays through the `World` interface with the minimal `HealEvent` only; `HealReminders` is the hydration reminder source; `HealImport` reads Health Connect and Huawei Health's pages on request (provider-neutral `Imported`, provenance, dedupe; since 0.186.0 also `StepsRecord` and `VitalReading` in `heal_steps`/`heal_vital` (schema 11), steps and the once-a-day readings update in place, `HealStore.payStepsAndImports`, `HealKind.STEPS`/`IMPORT`, `HealSettings.paidMarks`; `AndroidHealthConnect` in androidApp, heal/HealConnections.kt, 0.162.0; no Huawei metric is claimed) and `Integrations` (app-core) lists each module's integrations with class and status; its screens are `heal/Heal.kt` (Modules → Heal). The Heal Region is `tools/species/regions/heal.txt`; Heal's party is `PartyIds.HEAL`. **Heal's records are in `heal_*` tables from the start (schema 7, 0.136.0, `HealTables`, `LiveDocuments.moveHealToTables`), one family per private kind, never in the `save` document, and sealed since 0.154.0 (AES-256-GCM, its own Android Keystore key: `HealTables.seal` once at the first start with `DataStore.healCipher`, `KeystoreHealKey`; a sealed Heal with no key fails closed; backups and kept-aside copies are sealed; the passphrase backup seals Heal with Mint).**
- `mint-core/`: the Mint module's rules (docs/pokelife `MODULES/MINT.md` section 22): accounts, transactions, budgets, bills, reconciliation and review, exact integer-minor-unit money (`Money`), net position, available money, and what may pay Ledger Research (`MintRules`); a cosmetic account card style (`CardStyle`, `FinancialAccount.card`, 0.200.0, ids saved, colours in the UI); JVM and wasmJs, no UI, knows nothing about Pokémon or rewards (package `app.pokeplan.mint`). `MintStore` in app-core keeps its own save (`MintData`) and pays through the `World` interface with the minimal `MintEvent` only (never a figure); its screens are `mint/Mint.kt` (Modules → Mint). The Mint Region is `tools/species/regions/mint.txt`; Mint's party is `PartyIds.MINT`. **Mint's records are in `mint_*` tables from the start (schema 8, 0.141.0, `MintTables`, `LiveDocuments.moveMintToTables`), never in the `save` document, and every row and every backup copy of Mint is sealed (AES-256-GCM, Android Keystore key: `RowCipher`, `AesGcmCipher`, `KeystoreMintKey`, `DataStore.mintCipher`; tables refuse to start without it).** Mint also imports a statement CSV the player chooses (`StatementImport`, `MintStore.importStatement`, mint/MintImport.kt, 0.162.0), and has a passphrase-sealed portable backup (`PassphraseSealer`, `BackupBundle.portable`), bill reminders (`MintReminders`) and an optional screen-lock gate (`DeviceGate`) since 0.142.0. A new Pokémon arrives with the scene of its module (`Acquisition`, `Cutscenes.kt`): the egg only for Plan's eggs and a visitor's gift.
- `base-core/` (package `app.pokeplan.base`): the Base module's rules, renamed from Home on 13 November 2026 (docs/pokelife `MODULES/BASE.md` section 16): chores and their deterministic schedules (`Schedules`, `BaseRules.view`: only the latest occurrence up to today, no backlog), supplies with exact quantities (`Quantity`, thousandths), assets, maintenance, warranties, reference notes, search, and what may pay Habitat Discovery (`BaseRules.chorePays`); JVM and wasmJs, no UI, knows nothing about Pokémon or rewards (package `app.pokeplan.base`). `BaseStore` in app-core keeps its own save (`BaseData`) and pays through the `World` interface with the minimal `BaseEvent` only (never a household word); its screens are `base/Base.kt` (Modules → Base; `Place.HOUSEHOLD` and `Module.HOME` keep their stored names, `AppModel.base`). The Base Region is `tools/species/regions/household.txt` (`Region.HOUSEHOLD`, a stored name); Base's party is `PartyIds.BASE` (stored as `home`). **Base's records are in `home_*` tables from the start (schema 9, 0.143.0, `BaseTables`, `LiveDocuments.moveBaseToTables`), never in the `save` document; not sealed.** **Secret Base room (0.196.0, `Room.kt`, BASE.md 9.5):** `Decorations` (the 42-sprite catalogue, the Decoration meter's numbers, the three starters, a pick drawn from an event id), `RoomGrid` (the 44 cells), `RoomLook.forType` (the lead's primary type picks tree, shrub or one of four caves), `Placement` and `Room` (place, move, remove, tidy, where the party stands); the room's placements are `BaseData.room` (table `home_room`, schema 12), which decorations are owned is shared state in `AppData.decorations` (`GameStore.grantDecoration`, the meter `home-decor-meter`, `Reward.Decoration`). The screen is `composeApp/.../ui/base/BaseRoom.kt` (a Room card in Base's menu).
- `link-core/`: the Link module's rules (docs/pokelife `MODULES/LINK.md` section 15): people and notes, important dates (`LinkRules.next`: annual or once, 29 February observed on the 28th in a year without one), follow-ups (only the title is required; `LinkRules.state`: Upcoming or Due, never a warning), search, duplicate suggestions that never merge, and what may pay Postcard Trail (`LinkRules.pays`); JVM and wasmJs, no UI, knows nothing about Pokémon or rewards (package `app.pokeplan.link`). `LinkStore` in app-core keeps its own save (`LinkData`) and pays through the `World` interface with the minimal `LinkEvent` only (never a person or a follow-up word); its screens are `link/Link.kt` (Modules → Link; `Place.LINK`, `Module.LINK`, `AppModel.link`). The Link Region is `tools/species/regions/link.txt` (`Region.LINK`); Link's party is `PartyIds.LINK`. Contacts import (0.201.0): `ContactImport` (link-core: `parseBirthday`, `plan`, `fill`), `Person.sourceId`, `LinkStore.planImport`/`importContacts`; `ContactSource` (composeApp, `AppModel.contacts`) with `AndroidContacts` (androidApp `platform/`) and `ui/link/ContactImportView.kt`. Link's reminders (important dates 0, 1, 3 or 7 days before, follow-ups on their day; all off until switched on) are `LinkReminders`/`LinkReminderAdapter` on the shared scheduler (0.149.0). **Link's records are in `link_*` tables from the start (schema 10, 0.145.0, `LinkTables`, `LiveDocuments.moveLinkToTables`), never in the `save` document; not sealed.**
- `world-core/`: the World game's rules (docs/pokelife/MODULES/WORLD.md): dungeons, expedition teams, the deterministic offline simulation, Exploration Finds, rank and the story as data; JVM and wasmJs, no UI. Its save is `AppData.world`, kept by `GameStore`'s `world*` functions; its screens are `world/World.kt` (Modules → World). Not the same as the Explore module (trips and a travel journal) in docs/pokelife/FEATURES/POKEMON_ECOSYSTEM.md.
- `data/`: the local database that will replace the JSON saves (docs/pokelife `ARCHITECTURE/DATA_AND_MIGRATIONS.md`, REP-008): SQLDelight on SQLite, Android and JVM targets only, package `app.pokeplan.data`. Schema in `src/commonMain/sqldelight/.../Data.sq`; every released schema is committed in `databases/N.db`, and `jvmTest` runs `verifyCommonMainDataDbMigration`, so a schema change needs a numbered `.sqm` migration. `DataStore` (foreign keys checked on open, one transaction coordinator), `PokemonRepository`, `EconomyRepository` (idempotent, append-only ledger) (`LegacyJsonImporter` was removed in 0.175.0: only its tests used it). **Live since 0.129.0 on Android (the cutover):** the app's two JSON documents (`save`, `care`) live in the table `live_document` (`LiveDocuments`, schema v2), activated once from `save.json` and `care.json` (which are then never written again, kept as the rollback copy), with a daily file backup of the documents (`LiveBackup`, `filesDir/backups/`, newest 3) and a move-aside-and-restore path for a corrupt file; the Android wiring is `androidApp/.../LiveStore.kt` behind `saveStorage()`/`careStorage()`. The normalized tables (Pokémon, Bag, ledger) were filled by the importer only until 0.135.0 and are now the live authority for the player's side of the save (below); desktop and web keep their JSON stores. The shared reminder feature stores its snoozes and repeats in `reminder_hold` (schema v3). **PLAN's records live in their own tables since 0.133.0 (schema v5):** `PlanTables` under `LiveDocuments`, which splits the `save` document into PLAN's part (`plan_*` tables) and the rest (the `save` row) and puts it back together on a read; `movePlanToTables` activates it once with read-back. **Care's records live in their own tables since 0.132.0 (schema v4, REP-014):** `CareTables` takes the Care JSON apart into `care_*` rows and puts it back together under the unchanged `CareStore`, and `LiveDocuments` routes the `care` document through them after `moveCareToTables` (read back, old row kept aside); **The shared Pokémon, Bag and ledger tables are live since 0.135.0 (schema 6, REP-014):** `PokemonTables` splits the player's part of the `save` document (the Pokémon, their places, the Dex history, the Egg meter and trainer points, the Bag and the Poké balance) from the rest, `movePokemonToTables` activates it once (the save first goes through `GameStore.normalizeDocument`, which `LiveStore` sets as `LiveDocuments.normalizer`; read-back compared, the old save kept as `aside:save:pokemon:<millis>`, the balances one opening-balance event), a Pokémon's place is at most one row of `pokemon_place` (primary key = the Pokémon; WORLD teams and GEAR helpers stay in the `save` row and the write refuses a Pokémon named there and in the table), the ledger is the only writer of the balance and the Bag (a live write settles the change of one save as one event), and `WorldData.poke` is never stored. The app still reads one `AppData` through the split; the typed repositories are not used by it yet (`DATA_AND_MIGRATIONS.md` section 7.3.0). HEAL, MINT and HOME are on their own tables too (schemas 7 to 9); next: the typed repositories per module and the backup archive (`docs/pokelife/IMPLEMENTATION_GUIDE.md` section 3.2). Debug builds have a second icon, DB check (`DeviceSelfCheck`). Tests: `./gradlew :data:jvmTest`.
- `plan-core/`: PokéPlan's own logic (focus sessions, tasks, recurrence and frequency targets, lists, events, goals, the weekly review, reminders and nudges). Depends on `pokemon-core`. Package `app.pokeplan.plan`.
- `assets/`: real game assets (sprites, animated sprites, cries, sound effects, scenes, item icons, type plates, PC box wallpapers, fonts), fetched by the scripts in `tools/sprites` and listed with their sources and licences in `docs/assets.md`. How each is fetched, regenerated and used, the Dex data generator and the module region curation kit: the `game-assets` skill.
- `docs/design.md`: product and game design. `docs/economy.md`: every reward source, sink and cap, under `RewardRules.VERSION` (bump it, and update that file, with any rule that changes what an event pays). `docs/pokelife/FEATURES/POKEMON_ECOSYSTEM.md`: superseded module plan (see `docs/pokelife/README.md`), kept for the effort-event contract between them and the shared world, the Care module spec and the roadmap; its section 12 holds the adopted expansion blueprint (eight regions, one reward owner per action, Pokégear, the Android companion layer) and the 0.52.0 audit's work list; the launcher comes first in the roadmap.
- `legacy-android/`: the original Android Java app, no longer developed. Everything about it (commands, Firebase, architecture, the old rules) is in the `legacy-android` skill.
- `app-core/`: the app's rules engine with no UI (`GameStore` applies every user action with the real game rules and saves one JSON document through a `Storage`; `AppData` is the saved shape). JVM and wasmJs; its tests run with `./gradlew :app-core:jvmTest`.
- **No cloud (0.130.0):** Supabase, accounts, sync, the friends board, the server reward function, the JavaScript build of the engine and `tools/server` are gone (REP-012); the git history before 0.130.0 has them. Rewards are decided on the phone.
- The bottom tabs are **Home / Modules / Pokémon / Me** (`Tab` in `App.kt`). Home, Pokémon and Me hold what every module shares; each life module is a section of Modules (`screens/Modules.kt`: `Module`, Plan's `PlanSection` with Planner, Focus, Growth, Goals and Review), and `AppModel.module` keeps the open one. `Place` is where the app can be sent (widgets, shortcuts and notifications name `PLANNER`, `FOCUS` and `CARE`).
- Each tab or module with several sections (Modules, Plan, Planner, Pokémon, Me) opens on a **menu** of big cards with real item icons (`screens/Menu.kt`: `MenuGrid`, `SectionScaffold` with Back and the system back gesture), not tab pills.
- `composeApp/`: the shared Compose Multiplatform UI (targets: Android library, desktop JVM, wasmJs web), package `app.pokeplan.ui`; `App()` in `commonMain` is the entry point. Sprites (`assets/sprites`) and the Pokémon DS-era font (`assets/fonts`: `Pokemon-DP` and `-Bold` from the MIT-licensed hexparrot/pokemon-assets package for titles, buttons and numbers, used for body text too; the dialogue bitmap font `pmd-dialogue` (John2k4) for titles, labels and buttons) are copied into Compose resources by the `prepareComposeAssets` task; the generated `Res` is in package `pokeplan.composeapp.generated.resources`. Screens are in `screens/`, the GBA-style theme and components (`TextBox`, `PixelButton`) in `theme/`. `ScreenshotTest` renders screens offscreen to `composeApp/build/screenshots/*.png` (`./gradlew :composeApp:jvmTest --tests "*ScreenshotTest.<screen>*"`; plain `jvmTest` skips it, `-Pscreenshots` renders all), so a screen can be looked at without a device.
- `composeApp/.../ui/launcher/DungeonFloor.kt`, `TownFloor.kt` (REP-052, LAUNCHER.md 4.5): the launcher town as a Mystery Dungeon floor. `DungeonFloor.build` lays rooms and corridors over every page from `TownTiles.seed` (scene, date, `LauncherPrefs.layoutRoll`), with ponds, wall lakes, stairs, hidden `FloorTrap`s and the day's find spots; `path` is an eight-way A* (widget squares cost `SOFT_COST`), `Mover` lets Water and Flying types into water and only Ghost types through walls; the paths and the tile pictures are worked out off the main thread. `Autotile` maps a square's neighbours to a column of `ex-NNN-auto.png`. `FloorBackdrop` draws it, `FloorThings` the stairs, found traps and finds (`GameStore.townFinds`, `collectTownFind`; rules in `TownFinds`); `Town`'s `Resident` walks it with `roamFloor` and `spring`. Scenes keep the old ground band and `TownLayout`.
- `androidApp/`: the thin Android application (launcher icon is the legacy app's egg-with-check adaptive icon; `MainActivity` calls `App()` (after `LiveStore.recoveryPending` has been answered by `RecoveryChoice`, REP-026 c), passing the accelerometer-based `AccelerometerMotion` for Deep Focus and an optional start tab); Every module's reminders (Plan's tasks, streak reminder and weekly recap, Care, Heal, Mint, Home, Link) go through `reminders/SharedReminders.kt` (it plans three days ahead and a nightly 04:00 alarm plans again) (policy in `app-core/.../ReminderPolicy.kt`, `ReminderAdapters.kt`; REP-011); Glance widgets in `widgets/` (Today's tasks with rank badges and a tick; Buddy in its scene with the real idle animation (a ViewFlipper of frames through `AndroidRemoteViews`), tap to pet and a berry button; Focus showing the running session; Care with today's routines and a tick, through `careStore()`; `WidgetArt` builds their bitmaps from the real sprites, items and wallpapers) read and write the same save (the database since 0.129.0) as the app through `widgetStore()`, a task finished from a widget is paid when the app next opens, and the app calls `GameStore.reload()` when it comes back to the front so it never overwrites a widget's change; AGP 9, compileSdk 37, targetSdk 36, minSdk 26. Needs an Android SDK (`sdk.dir` in the git-ignored `local.properties`, or `ANDROID_HOME`) with `platforms;android-37.0` and build tools. `composeApp` sets `androidResources { enable = true }`, without which the APK ships without sprites, icons or fonts. Tests (REP-033, 0.175.1): `src/test` runs on the JVM with Robolectric (`./gradlew :androidApp:testDebugUnitTest`, CI's Android step): `AndroidTestBase` (fresh app folder, `LiveStore.forgetForTests()` as a process death), `FakeAndroidKeyStore` (an in-memory `AndroidKeyStore` JCA provider, `available = false` for the fail-closed paths), `LiveStoreTest`, `reminders/SharedRemindersTest`, `widgets/WidgetSaveTest`; Robolectric fetches its Android jars from the Google mirror of Maven Central (`robolectric.dependency.repo.url` in `androidApp/build.gradle.kts`).

- Backup off the phone (REP-034, 0.199.0): `data/javaMain/OffPhoneBackup.kt` (verified copies into a `BackupFolder`, newest 7), `androidApp/OffPhone.kt` (folder, Keystore-sealed passphrase, daily `OffPhone.Worker`), `SafBackupFolder.kt` (Storage Access Framework), `AndroidBackups` (the folder picker), `ui/screens/Settings.kt` `OffPhoneSection`.
