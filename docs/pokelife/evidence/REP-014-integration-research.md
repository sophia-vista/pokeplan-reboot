# REP-014: HEAL and MINT provider research (desk research, 8 November 2026)

*This is desk research from public web pages, done in a cloud session with no watch, no phone and no bank account. **Nothing here was tested end to end**, so by `ARCHITECTURE/INTEGRATIONS_AND_PRIVACY.md` sections 1 and 17 nothing in it makes a provider "supported". It records what is documented, what is only claimed by third parties, and what only the owner can do. Several sources are vendor marketing pages; they are marked.*

## 1. Huawei Health and the Huawei Watch Fit 4 Pro (HEAL.md 10.1, INTEGRATIONS 5.2)

Order of investigation from the spec, and what was found:

| # | Route | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Health Connect interoperability | No official statement that Huawei Health writes into Health Connect. A TechRadar review lists "No Google Health Connect sync (Android)" as a drawback of the Fit 4 Pro; a help article and a vendor blog say the same ("no native support"). None gives a per-metric list. | **Unverified, probably absent.** Only a test on the owner's phone settles it: after installing the Health Connect app (or on Android 14+) and enabling any sharing in Huawei Health, does `com.huawei.health` appear under Health Connect → App permissions / Data and access, and does Heal's "Imported records" ever list it? |
| 2 | Huawei Health Kit / Health Service Kit | Exists and is official. Needs a Huawei developer account, an app registered in AppGallery Connect and **Huawei's approval per app** (a company or app review may be asked for); access is by user OAuth consent with a scope per data type (e.g. heart rate and sleep are separate scopes). Documented limits: the latest heart rate is available only if continuous monitoring is on in Huawei Health, otherwise only manual measurements; sleep is historical records only; every call depends on the user having enabled health monitoring. A REST form for partners exists (also AppGallery Connect and review). | **Blocked on the owner**: nobody can register or apply from a session. Until approved there is no key, no scope list and no proof it is open to an individual app in the Philippines. HEAL must not depend on HMS Core, AppGallery or a Huawei account. |
| 3 | Huawei Health export | Not researched to a source: no document found that names a format, history depth or stable ids. | **Unverified.** Needs the owner to request their data from Huawei's privacy centre and share a sample (with personal values removed) so the format can be read. |
| 4 | Maintained third-party bridge | **Health Sync** (a third-party app) is described by its own site and by help articles as reading Huawei Health and writing to Google Fit or Health Connect, one way for most data (writing back to Huawei Health is limited to weight, blood pressure and blood sugar). Per-metric coverage for the Fit 4 Pro is not documented; some sources are vendor pages. | **Optional, unofficial, unverified.** It would feed the Health Connect import HEAL already has, so it needs no code here. HEAL never depends on it (spec 5.2, item 4). |
| 5 | Direct Bluetooth or the proprietary protocol; reading Huawei's cloud with a login | Open-source projects exist that read the Huawei Health cloud with a browser-assisted login. | **Rejected by the spec** (no credential scraping, no reverse engineering): not built, not suggested in the app. |

**What this means for the app.** HEAL can already read whatever reaches Health Connect, and it shows the writing app of every imported record, so route 1 and route 4 need no further code. A direct Huawei connection needs a developer approval (route 2) that only the owner can start; building an adapter before it exists would be guessing at scopes. The Huawei card in Heal → Connections therefore says what is true: not verified, the two routes that could work, and what has to be tested.

**Matrix still to be filled on the real watch, phone and Huawei account (Philippine region), one metric at a time:** steps and distance, workouts, heart rate, resting heart rate, HRV, sleep and stages, stress, calories, SpO2 and weight; for each the route, permission, history depth, source timestamp behaviour, sync delay, units, region and firmware limits, cost and maintenance risk (HEAL.md 10.1). HEAL only has records for workouts, sleep, weight, body fat and drinks, so those five come first.

## 2. Philippine providers for MINT (INTEGRATIONS 7.1, MINT.md 10)

| Provider | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **UnionBank** | Has an API developer portal since 2017 (a 2019 report counts over 600 APIs: bills payment, funds transfer, inquiries, authentication, a payment gateway and others). It is aimed at fintechs and businesses; the "individual customers" in reports are the bank's customers, not individual developers. **No source found** on personal-account transaction history for an individual developer's app, OAuth consent flows, or current terms; the sources are mostly from 2019. | **Unverified.** The owner would need to open the portal, see whether an individual can register, and which APIs return a customer's own transactions with the customer's consent. |
| **GCash, Maya, GoTyme, MariBank** | No public documentation found of an API that gives a user's own transaction history to a third-party app. A vendor page lists all four as "integrations" but describes "protocol analysis" and "interface reconstruction" (reverse engineering), which the spec forbids and which is not evidence of sanctioned access. | **Unverified; treat as unsupported.** |
| **BSP Open Finance framework** | BSP Circular 1122 (2021) sets a framework for permissioned data sharing by BSP-supervised institutions with authorized third-party providers (Account Information and Payment Initiation Service Providers), and a 2026 vendor article says it is expanding. **No official list of live participants or endpoints was found**, and it is unclear whether an individual developer's personal-use app can qualify as an authorized provider. | **Watch, do not build.** If it becomes real, the route is an official, consented API (INTEGRATIONS 7.2, item 4), never scraping. |
| Notification reading | Android's `NotificationListenerService` reads every app's notifications. | **Not built**: INTEGRATIONS 7.3 puts it behind a separate privacy review. |

**Conclusion.** The statement file (CSV, built in 0.162.0) stays MINT's only integration. No provider adapter is built because no provider has a verified, consented, individual-developer route. **Next evidence the owner can supply:** (1) whether UnionBank's portal lets an individual register and which of its APIs return a customer's own transactions; (2) which statement formats GCash, Maya, GoTyme and MariBank let the user export (CSV, PDF or none), with one sample each (values removed), so the reader's column guesses can be tested against real headers; (3) whether the BSP lists any participant an individual can use.
