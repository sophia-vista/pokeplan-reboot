# World story and scene engine

Status: built. `docs/pokelife/MODULES/WORLD.md` 4.4 owns the story runtime. This file says what exists, how to author a scene, and what is still to do.

## What exists

- **Model** (`world-core`, package `app.pokeplan.world.story`): `StoryEpisode` (a chapter's scenes), `StoryScene` (`SceneType` DIALOGUE, STAGE, DUNGEON or CINEMATIC; a background or, for a dungeon walk, the `dungeonId` whose own tiles it is drawn on; a cast; an event script), `StoryCharacter` and a typed `SceneEvent` set: `Show`, `Hide`, `Express`, `Face`, `Move` (a path of grid cells), `Say` (a line, or narration when the speaker is null), `Wait`, `Fade`, `Background`, `Item`, `TakeItem`, `Emote`, `Flag`, `End`.
- **Runtime** (`SceneState`): what each event leaves behind (who is where, what lies on the floor, the line on screen). `SceneState.finalOf(scene)` applies every event at once, which is what Skip lands on, so a skipped scene is never left half done. `StoryScene.problems()` lists mistakes in a script (a speaker not in the cast, someone used before being shown, a line over 160 characters, no End); the tests run it over every authored scene.
- **Player** (`composeApp`, `ui/world/StoryPlayer.kt`): a dialogue view (the place with the speakers standing in it as sprites, and the speaker's small framed Mystery Dungeon portrait at its side, over the scene, above the dark dialogue box with the speaker's name in yellow), and a stage or dungeon view (a ten by six grid of cells; on a ground map the grid is a window of 40-pixel cells centred on the map and a camera shows the map at a whole-number pixel scale, about 256 pixels of the art across, following the speaker, with sprites at that same scale: `MapStage`; on a stock scene or a dungeon's own tiles the grid fills a ten by six box; sprites drawn at their cells and walking with their own walk animations, items on the floor, an exclamation mark over a head). Tap to advance (a tap while text is typing completes it), Skip scene, a close button, a typewriter that Reduce motion turns off. A scene watched to its end is recorded in `WorldData.scenesDone`, a finished episode in `episodesDone` (a record for the archive; replaying grants nothing).
- **The companion** is always the player's buddy (`BUDDY` in a script; whoever is chosen, portrait and sprite from their own form). Text may use `{buddy}` (the buddy's nickname) and `{player}` (the user's name, the Witness; not a Pokémon). A character with no portrait for an expression shows its normal face.
- **Where it plays:** a chapter with an authored episode (`StoryCatalog.episodeFor(layer, chapter)`) plays it when the chapter opens and from the Story archive's play button; a chapter with none would play its title card (a test checks that every chapter of every layer has an episode).
- **Authored:** the opening, Mew's Unofficial Welcome (`IntroMewChapter`, seven scenes, belongs to no layer), the Rescue Team arc (`RescueChapters`, 6 chapters, and `RescuePostStory`, episodes 101 to 105), the Explorers arc (`ExplorersChaptersA` and `B`, 8 chapters, and `ExplorersPostStory`, 101 to 106) and five chapters each for Kanto, Johto, Hoenn, Sinnoh, Unova, Kalos, Alola, Galar, Paldea and Hisui; `StoryCatalog` lists them all. A readable copy is generated by `./gradlew :world-core:storyScript`; the brief for the unported writing is `docs/world-story-rewrite.md`.

## How to write a chapter

Add a function to a layer's chapters object (see `RescueChapters`) that returns a `StoryEpisode`, and list it in `StoryCatalog`. A line is `Say("pelly", "Special delivery!", HAPPY)`; narration is `Say(null, "...")`. A walk is `Move(BUDDY, listOf(3.6f to 3.4f))` (cells across and down; a dungeon floor row is about y = 2.6 to 3.4). `StoryEngineTest` fails if a script is unsound.

## Art

Portraits, walking sprites, scene backgrounds and dungeon tiles are the existing SpriteCollab and game assets (the project's real-assets-only rule); nothing in the engine depends on that choice, a different asset source would only change the resolvers.

## Also built

- **Cinematic scenes** (`SceneType.CINEMATIC`): black bars, characters whose id starts with `big_` drawn large, `Shake`, `Flash`, `Letterbox`; Reduce motion skips the motion.
- **Sound:** `Cry(who)` plays a Pokémon's real cry and `Sound(name)` any game effect from the app's list. There is no music yet.
- **Choices and flags:** `Choice(prompt, options)` (two or three answers that set a flag; skipped, the first is taken) and `Branch(flag, events)`. Flags are kept in `WorldData.storyFlags` and carry into later scenes; they are for callbacks only and never gate a reward or an unlock.
- **Resume:** `WorldData.storyResume` holds the next scene's index per episode; a watched episode replays from its start.
- **People you have met** in the Story archive, from the casts of the scenes watched (`StoryCatalog.peopleMet`).
- **Every chapter written**, with the writing aid in `ScriptDsl.kt` (`dialogue`, `stage`, `dungeon`, `cinematic` blocks of `say`, `walk`, `shake`, `choice`...). A test checks that every chapter of every layer has an episode with the chapter's number and title, and that every script is sound.

## Not built yet

- Music and ambience (no music assets in the project); a particle-effects set beyond shake and flash.
- A Tiled map pipeline and a story asset manifest: scenes use the existing dungeon tile sheets, scene images and the ground maps named by `StoryPlaces` (`map:<name>`).
- A scene preview tool beyond the Story archive and the screenshot test; a character index with lore text.
- The rewrite of the first-pass writing for Explorers 5 to 8, the Mythical follow-ups and the regions (`docs/world-story-rewrite.md`): scripts only, no engine work.
