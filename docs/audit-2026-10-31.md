# Audit of 31 October 2026: what was inspected, what was found, what is next

Answers `pokelife_repository_audit_claude_code.md` (the owner's audit brief). Per its section 21 this is first a report of the
**actual** behaviour, with severity (P0 correctness and safety, P1 core usability, P2 depth, P3 polish), then a phased plan, then
the decisions only the owner can make. Nothing here changes product direction, rewards, regions or saved data.

## What was inspected, and what was not

Inspected in code (this session, 0.83 to 0.89 and this review): the whole World module (`world-core`, `screens/World.kt`,
`WorldArt.kt`, its `GameStore` functions, widget, economy simulation), navigation entry points (`Place`, `AppModel.open`,
the Android shortcut and widget extras), the launcher settings and its pages, the pixel text renderer, token use in the new screens.

**Not inspected in depth, so nothing below claims anything about them:** the Plan screens and `plan-core` rules beyond what the
economy docs state, Care's reminder and notification parity, Care and main-save sync conflicts, the Supabase backend, TalkBack
behaviour on a device, widget refresh reliability on a device, Pokégear tools other than their reward ownership (docs only),
the Pokémon collection screens' performance with a large box. They are the next audit passes (Phase 1 below).

### Rules the audit stresses, and where World stands

- *One reward owner per action:* World's jobs, dungeons and research are all World's; the shop and Dojo only spend. A recruited
  Pokémon enters the shared collection once (`worldRecruit` removes the find). No other module pays for a World action.
- *No decay, no expiry, no streak loss:* true. Jobs have cooldowns (1, 7 and 14 days) but never expire; missed days cost nothing.
- *No second inventory:* World stages items in `AppData.world.items` and moves them to the Bag by an explicit button. That is a
  second place items can sit; the audit asks for one authoritative inventory (finding 6 and section 8.9). **OWNER DECISION**:
  pay straight into the Bag, or keep the staging area as the "Finds" moment.
- *Economy:* the audit says "do not nerf acquisition". This session cut treasure-box Rare Candies and job pay after the simulation
  showed hundreds of candies a year; both numbers are still generous (about 0.3 to 0.7 candies a day from World). The owner
  should confirm this is the intended level.

## Phased plan (from section 18, trimmed to what the code shows)

1. **Phase 1, finish the audit:** navigation chains (Back inside modules) as tests; a map of every action to its event, owner
   and ledger entry for Plan and Care (the economy doc has the table; check the code against it); save migration tests with an
   old save; export and erase coverage test; a search for unused composables and deprecated APIs.
2. **Phase 2, shared consistency:** token sweep (raw dp and colours outside `Spacing` and `Pixel`), 48 dp targets, empty and
   first-use states for every landing page, 130% text for Plan, Care and the Pokémon screens (World is done).
3. **Phase 3, Plan usability** (Quick Add, Inbox vs Today, timeline alternatives to drag) and **Phase 4, Care** (reminder parity,
   privacy and sync tests). Larger; each deserves its own session.
4. **Phase 5, World depth:** pre-expedition summary (why this team suits this dungeon), an expedition log of finds, a next-step
   hint on the landing, story text and a Guardian scene, move-based gates once the Dex has moves.
5. **Phase 6 and 7:** Pokémon screens, widgets, Pokégear, watch and wallpaper feasibility, then the Growth/Grow naming and the
   future-module admission checklist.
