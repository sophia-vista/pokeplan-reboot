# Audit of 31 October 2026: what was inspected, what was found, what is next

Answers `pokelife_repository_audit_claude_code.md` (the owner's audit brief). Per its section 21 this is first a report of the
**actual** behaviour, with severity (P0 correctness and safety, P1 core usability, P2 depth, P3 polish), then a phased plan, then
the decisions only the owner can make. Nothing here changes product direction, rewards, regions or saved data.

## What was inspected, and what was not

Inspected in code (this session, 0.83 to 0.89 and this review): the whole World module (`world-core`, `screens/World.kt`,
`WorldArt.kt`, its `GameStore` functions, widget, economy simulation), navigation entry points (`Place`, `AppModel.open`,
the Android shortcut and widget extras), the launcher settings and its pages, the pixel text renderer, token use in the new screens.

**Not inspected in depth, so nothing below claims anything about them:** the Plan screens and `plan-core` rules beyond what the
economy docs state, Care's reminder and notification parity, Care and main-save sync conflicts, the Supabase backend, TalkBack
behaviour on a device, widget refresh reliability on a device, Pokégear tools other than their reward ownership (docs only),
the Pokémon collection screens' performance with a large box. They are the next audit passes (Phase 1 below).

### Rules the audit stresses, and where World stands

- *One reward owner per action:* World's jobs, dungeons and research are all World's; the shop and Dojo only spend. A recruited
  Pokémon enters the shared collection once (`worldRecruit` removes the find). No other module pays for a World action.
- *No decay, no expiry, no streak loss:* true. Jobs have cooldowns (1, 7 and 14 days) but never expire; missed days cost nothing.
- *No second inventory:* World stages items in `AppData.world.items` and moves them to the Bag by an explicit button. That is a
  second place items can sit; the audit asks for one authoritative inventory (finding 6 and section 8.9). Decided and done in 0.90.0: items go straight into the Bag
  (and the reward log); World keeps no stock of its own (a save from before is emptied into the Bag at the next settle).
- *Economy:* the audit says "do not nerf acquisition". This session cut treasure-box Rare Candies and job pay after the simulation
  showed hundreds of candies a year; both numbers are still generous (about 0.3 to 0.7 candies a day from World). The owner
  should confirm this is the intended level.

### What Phase 1 inspected, and what it found

- **Save compatibility:** `SaveCompatibilityTest` removes each top-level field of a current save in turn and decodes it: only
  `player` is required, so every field added since has a default and an old save loads. A save from before World, the reward
  history and the newer settings loads with their defaults; `Region.PLAN` still stores as `"HOME"`; a log with the new reward kind
  round-trips.
- **Export and erase:** the export (`cloudDocument`) holds World's save and the reward history (it leaves out only the account,
  egg tickets and chosen calendars); `eraseDevice` returns both to their defaults (tested). Care's save is exported and erased by
  its own store (`CareStore.exportJson`, `erase`), as documented.
- **Reward owners:** the functions that grant items, Pokémon or points are `start`, `achieve` (tasks), `care` (Care events),
  `completeSubtask`, `completeMilestone`, `completeReview`, `completeMiniQuest`, `claimWelcomeBack`, `claimQuest`, `settleFocus`,
  `magicGuard`, `befriend` (Care), `grant` (a server gift) and `grantPokemon`, which the Guardian and recruiting call; the spenders
  are `buyRareCandies`, `transfer`, `takeItem`, and World's `worldBuyCandy`. No screen grants directly: every one is a `GameStore`
  function. Existing tests show a task, a routine and a skill never pay twice; the other guards were not each tested here.
- **Unreferenced code:** of 1,335 functions only five are referenced nowhere: `PmdButton` and `NamePlate` (unused composables,
  **removed**), `Items.removeAll` and `Items.asContext` (core API kept for the other apps of the family), and
  `CareStore.updateRoutine`.
- **Finding 14 (P1):** `CareStore.updateRoutine` existed but no screen called it: **a Care routine could not be edited** (only added,
  archived or deleted). **Fixed in 0.90.1** (Edit in a routine's options; history is kept; tested).
- **Deprecations in code:** `.dayOfMonth` (now `.day`) was replaced where it could be; `.monthNumber` stays (the datetime version in
  use has no `Month.number`); `BackHandler` in the launcher wants `NavigationEventHandler`; `updateAppWidgetSize` is deprecated in
  the launcher activity. The Gradle scripts use the deprecated `js(IR)` and `androidLibrary`. None is urgent.
- **Back chains inside modules:** not tested (needs a Compose UI test harness; `BackHandler` calls live in composables). The place
  and menu logic is tested; this stays open.
