## `link-core/src/commonMain/kotlin/app/pokeplan/link/BaZi.kt`

### BaZi calendar rules

Documented in `docs/pokelife/MODULES/LINK.md 2.6`.

### BaZi.dayNumber epoch

`BaZi.dayNumber` counts civil days since 1900-01-01 using pure Gregorian arithmetic, so no date library is needed. It is the same epoch the `BaZiTables` solar-term minutes use.

### BaZi year turns at Lichun

Documented in `docs/pokelife/MODULES/LINK.md 2.6`.

### BaZi.chart time units

`BaZi.chart` takes the birth clock time as minutes after local midnight and the UTC offset as minutes the local clock is ahead of UTC (so Manila is +480). A null time gives a chart without the hour pillar.

### Beijing clock and unknown hour as noon

BaZi compares the birth instant with the solar-term tables on the Beijing clock (UTC+8). When the birth hour is unknown the instant is taken as noon, so the year, month and day pillars are still worked out and only the hour pillar is left out.

### BaZi month from the latest jie

The BaZi month is the latest jie at or before the instant. Before Xiaohan (the first jie of the calendar year) the month is still December's Daxue of the previous year, so the jie lookup starts from the year before.

### BaZi jie index to branch

The twelve jie in the tables run Xiaohan to Daxue. Xiaohan starts the Ox month, Lichun the Tiger month and so on to Daxue, which starts the Rat month, so the month branch is the jie index plus one, modulo twelve.

### Late Rat hour takes next day's stem

Documented in `docs/pokelife/MODULES/LINK.md 2.6`.

### Kua.lucky order

`Kua.lucky` lists the four auspicious directions in a fixed order: Sheng Qi, Tian Yi, Yan Nian, Fu Wei. `LoreChinese` reads their meanings by position, so the order must not change.

### Kua uses the BaZi year

The Kua number is computed from the BaZi year, not the calendar year, so someone born in January or before Lichun counts in the year before. It is null outside 1902 to 2098.

### Life Path reduction method

Life Path reduces the month, the day and the year separately (keeping 11, 22 and 33) and then adds them and reduces again, the usual method. For 1 November 1990 that is 11 + 1 + 1 = 13 = 4.

## `link-core/src/commonMain/kotlin/app/pokeplan/link/BaZiTables.kt`

### BaZiTables is generated

Documented in `docs/pokelife/MODULES/LINK.md 2.6`.

### BaZiTables.JIE_MINUTES format

`BaZiTables.JIE_MINUTES` holds, for each year, the twelve jie (Xiaohan, Lichun, Jingzhe, Qingming, Lixia, Mangzhong, Xiaoshu, Liqiu, Bailu, Hanlu, Lidong, Daxue) as minutes since 1900-01-01 00:00 Beijing time, one line per year. The file is generated by tools/link/generate_bazi.py.

### BaZiTables.NEW_YEAR format

`BaZiTables.NEW_YEAR` stores each year's Chinese New Year as month*100+day in the Gregorian calendar (219 is 19 February).

## `link-core/src/commonMain/kotlin/app/pokeplan/link/Birthplaces.kt`

### Birthplaces use standard time offsets

`Birthplaces` lists places with latitude, longitude and the standard UTC offset in minutes, not the daylight-saving offset; the user can edit the offset field to add daylight saving. The list is offline with no search, and a place not in it is entered as coordinates.

## `link-core/src/commonMain/kotlin/app/pokeplan/link/ContactImport.kt`

### ContactCard reads only needed fields

Documented in `docs/pokelife/MODULES/LINK.md 6.1`.

### ImportRow.matches

`ImportRow.matches` holds the existing people a contact may be (empty for a new one). A row of kind KNOWN holds the single person the contact was imported as earlier.

### ImportPlan.unnamed

`ImportPlan.unnamed` counts contacts with no name. They cannot become people, but the preview says how many there were instead of dropping them silently.

### Import never overwrites without a pick

Documented in `docs/pokelife/MODULES/LINK.md 6.1`.

### Contact birthday parsing

`ContactImport.parseBirthday` accepts Android's `yyyy-MM-dd`, `--MM-dd` (no year) and `--MMdd`. A year before 1900 or after 2200 is dropped but the day and month are kept, because phones store 1604 or 0000 for an unknown year. Anything that is not a real day gives null and the person is imported without a date.

### ContactImport.plan matching

`ContactImport.plan` marks a contact KNOWN when its source id was imported before, MAYBE when its name, any phone or any email matches someone already in LINK, and NEW otherwise. Nameless contacts are counted, not listed, and duplicate offers of the same contact collapse to one row.

### LinkData persistence

`LinkData` is one document that `LinkTables` (in the data module) splits into one table family per kind of record. It holds nothing about Pokémon or rewards.

### LinkKinds table names

Each `LinkKinds` entry is stored in its own table family (`link_person`, `link_note`, `link_date`, `link_followup`), so the kind can be exported or erased on its own. The names are stable because the schema and backups refer to them.

### Hit.id for a note

In a search `Hit`, `id` is the matched record's id, except that a note's hit carries its person's id, so tapping it opens the person.

### Upcoming.years

`Upcoming.years` is the age or anniversary number an important date marks on the day it is observed, and null when the date has no year. `day` is the observed day, which is 28 February for a 29 February date in a non-leap year.

### Slambook profile

Documented in `docs/pokelife/MODULES/LINK.md 2.6`.

### Profile enneagram sentinel

`Profile.enneagram` and `enneagramWing` hold 1 to 9, with 0 meaning none; the type is typed in by the user, never inferred.

### Profile love languages

`Profile.loveLanguages` keeps up to five distinct love languages, most meaningful first. `tidy` enforces the limit and drops repeats.

### Profile DISC

`Profile.disc` keeps one or two distinct DISC style letters, the main one first. `tidy` enforces the limit.

### Birth fields

`Birth.minutes` is the birth clock time as written on the record, in minutes after midnight, and `utcOffsetMinutes` is the UTC offset that clock was on. The birth date itself is not here: it is the person's Birthday date. An empty `kuaFormula` means none was chosen, and the Kua number is not shown.

Documented in `docs/pokelife/MODULES/LINK.md 2.6`.

### Sky.julianDay inputs

`Sky.julianDay` takes the minutes after midnight on the local clock and the UTC offset in minutes the local clock is ahead of UTC, and returns the Julian Day in UT.

### Sky.elements layout

`Sky.elements` holds JPL's 'Approximate positions of the planets' Table 1 (valid 1800 AD to 2050 AD) as twelve numbers per planet: a, e, I, L, longitude of perihelion, longitude of node, each followed by its change per century.

### Sky.helio frame

`Sky.helio` returns heliocentric x, y, z on the J2000 ecliptic, in astronomical units.

### Sky.angles return order

`Sky.angles` returns a pair of the rising degree (ascendant) first and the midheaven second, both as ecliptic longitudes in degrees.

## `link-core/src/commonTest/kotlin/app/pokeplan/link/BirthChartTest.kt`

### Sky vectors test goes through the public API

The vectors test rebuilds a calendar day and local clock from each reference Julian Day (UTC, offset 0) so that the public `Sky.chart` entry point is what is tested, not the internal Julian Day maths.

### civil date from Julian Day Number

The test helper `civil` turns a Julian Day Number back into a civil date with the Fliegel and Van Flandern algorithm, only to rebuild dates for the vectors test.

## `link-core/src/commonTest/kotlin/app/pokeplan/link/BirthVectors.kt`

### BirthVectors provenance

`BirthVectors` are reference values made by independent code, not by the code under test: Four Pillars and Chinese New Year years from the `lunar_python` package (Beijing clock, hour taken as written); planet, Sun and Moon longitudes from `ephem` (apparent ecliptic of date); the ascendant and midheaven from a formula checked against `ephem`. Regenerate only with a reason.

### BirthVectors.pillars layout

Each `BirthVectors.pillars` row is: year, month, day, minutes after midnight, then the year stem, year branch, month stem, month branch, day stem, day branch, hour stem and hour branch as indexes.

### BirthVectors.lunarYears layout

Each `BirthVectors.lunarYears` row is: year, month, day, and the Chinese (lunar) year that date falls in.

### BirthVectors.sky layout

Each `BirthVectors.sky` row is: Julian Day (UT), latitude, longitude east, ascendant, midheaven, then the longitudes of Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune and Pluto, in degrees.

### BirthVectors.kua layout

Each `BirthVectors.kua` row is: year, month, day (all after Lichun), formula (0 male, 1 female) and the expected Kua number.
