# Art and font sources

All art is real game assets, never generated (the owner's rule). This is a private project; check each source's terms before any public release.

| What | Where it is | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Pokémon sprites (front and shiny) | `assets/sprites/` | PokeAPI/sprites, `tools/sprites/fetch.py` |
| Item icons (bag, tab icons) | `assets/items/` | PokeAPI/sprites, `tools/sprites/fetch_items.py` |
| Mystery Dungeon sprites (preferred) | `assets/pmd/` | The [PMD SpriteCollab](https://github.com/PMDCollab/SpriteCollab) at commit `cf6c95f`: the official Pokémon Mystery Dungeon sprites (Chunsoft) and community sprites in the same style, for 961 species and 113 forms (916 with a shiny). `tools/sprites/fetch_pmd.py` keeps the front-facing row of each Idle animation (Walk where there is none), cut to the frames' box, as sprite sheets plus `pmd.tsv`; `credits.tsv` names each sprite's artists. The same goes for the Sleep (1074 forms), Hop (1074) and Eat (241) rows, in `sleep/`, `hop/` and `eat/` with their own tables, and the Walk rows facing down, to the side (right; mirrored for left) and up, in `walk-down/`, `walk-side/` and `walk-up/` (fetched alone with `fetch_pmd.py walk`; the launcher's town uses them), and `portraits/` holds the Normal, Happy, Joyous, Inspired, Determined and Surprised portraits of 1210 forms (973 with a shiny) as one strip each, with `portraits.tsv` naming their artists. **CC BY-NC 4.0: non-commercial use, with credit** (shown under each Pokédex entry). The app uses them wherever a Pokémon is drawn and falls back to the rows below. |
| Animated sprites | `assets/animated/` | Pokémon Black/White animated GIFs from PokeAPI/sprites (same pinned commit), converted frame by frame to sprite-sheet PNGs by `tools/sprites/fetch_animated.py`. Gen 1 to 5 only. Game Freak's art; private use only. |
| Cries | `assets/cries/` | The latest cries from [PokeAPI/cries](https://github.com/PokeAPI/cries) (pinned commit), `tools/sprites/fetch_cries.py`. Game Freak's audio; private use only. |
| Sound effects | `assets/sfx/` | The select, jump, heal and escape effects and the Pokémon Center healing jingle (`pokemon-center.ogg`) from [hexparrot/pokemon-assets](https://github.com/hexparrot/pokemon-assets) `assets/audio` (pinned commit, the same package as the display font), made mono Ogg by `tools/sprites/fetch_sfx.py` (needs ffmpeg). MIT for the package (licence next to them); the audio is Game Freak's. Private use only. | The launcher and UI sounds (`cursor`, `confirm`, `back`, `error`, `drawer-*`, `pin`, `level-up`, `egg-get` and the rest of the `Sfx` enum) are cut from the Mystery Dungeon: Red Rescue Team sound-effects dump by `tools/sprites/convert_pmd_sfx.py` (the dump is not in the repo; the owner named 27 of the clips, the rest were picked by length and pitch and may need swapping after a listen). Game Freak's audio, private use only.
| Scenes | `assets/scenes/` | Battle backdrops (city, Plan's town; meadow, Care's; and forest, route, mountain, volcano cave, beach, desert, thunder plains, ice cave, damp cave) from [smogon/pokemon-showdown-client](https://github.com/smogon/pokemon-showdown-client) `play.pokemonshowdown.com/fx` (pinned commit), `tools/sprites/fetch_scenes.py`. Game Freak style art by the Showdown artists; private use only. |
| World art | `assets/world/` | Mystery Dungeon pictures for the World screens: the town (`town`), the guild room (`guild-room`), the Wigglytuff guild summit (`summit`), the bone cave (`cave`). Cropped from sprite rips supplied by the owner (the town, rooms and cave by redblueyellow, "no credit needed"; the tileset sheets by SilverDeoxys563), from Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Sky and Rescue Team. Not fetched by a script: the crops are committed. Private use only. |
| World map, café and effects | `assets/world/map-1..8.png`, `cafe.png`, `fx/` | The Mystery Dungeon world map in eight stages of being uncovered (`map-N`; the ninth frame of the sheet, with the game's own dots, is not used), Spinda's Café room, and four attack-effect strips (`fx/hit`, `welcome`, `steam`, `sparkle`: frames of effects 0020, 0007, 0030 and 0094 of the Explorers of Sky move effects, laid side by side, sizes in `fx/fx.tsv`). Sprite rips supplied by the owner (the map and café by redblueyellow, "no credit needed"; the effects from The Spriters Resource's Explorers of Sky attack effects archive). Private use only. The archive's 258 effects stay out of the repo. |
| Mystery Dungeon 2 items | `assets/pmd-items/` | 214 item pictures (berries, seeds, gummis, orbs, scarves, ribbons, ranks, traps and more) cut from the Mystery Dungeon 2 complete items sheet by Null, ripped by redblueyellow ("no credit needed, but it's appreciated"), `pmd2-001` to `pmd2-214`, each a transparent square at its own size; `manifest.tsv` has where each came from. They are choices for the home-page icon of a pinned app (and drawn by `ItemIcon` from `files/pmditems`). Private use only. |
| Status icons | `assets/status/` | The Mystery Dungeon status icons ("!", "?", the red X, the Zz of sleep, the skull) from the status sheet the owner sent (`sheet.png`, the source), cut into 16 px frame strips by `tools/sprites/cut_status.py` and drawn by `StatusIcon`. Private use only. |
| Weather | `assets/weather/` | The Mystery Dungeon: Red Rescue Team weather effects (rain, snow, blizzard, sand, cloud shadows) from the sheets the owner sent (`source/`), cut by `tools/sprites/cut_weather.py` and drawn by `WeatherLayer`. Private use only. |
| Dungeon tiles | `assets/tiles/` | Tilesets of the Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Sky dungeons (26 of them, nine of them the owner's Tiny Woods, Thunderwave Cave, Mt. Steel, Waterfall Cave, Mt. Freeze, Wish Cave, Sky Tower, Temporal Tower and World Abyss sheets, which World's expedition screen builds its boxes from (the Silver Trench sheet is palette-cycled and not used); Lush Prairie, Apple Woods, Dusk Forest, Deep Dusk Forest, Drenched Bluff, Craggy Coast, Beach Cave, Brine Cave, Crystal Cave, Crystal Crossing, Dark Hill, Deep Sealed Ruin, Concealed Ruins, Amp Plains and their deep variants), ripped and laid out by SilverDeoxys563 on The Spriters Resource (credit not required, appreciated), art by Chunsoft; private use only. `assets/tiles/sheets/` holds the sheets as downloaded; `tools/tiles/cut_tiles.py` cuts each into a 6 x 4 atlas of 24 x 24 tiles (wall block, water block, ground and two alternates), only the sheets' own pixels. The launcher's town lays them out in rectangles (`TownLayout`). The crater and chasm sheets are not used (palette-cycled walls, no walls or water). |
| Dialogue font | `assets/fonts/pmd-dialogue.png`, `.tsv` | The Pokémon dialogue bitmap font (white letters with a black shadow), ripped by John2k4 from Game Freak's sprites; private use only. `tools/fonts/cut_pmd_font.py` measures each glyph into the `.tsv`. It has Latin letters (with é and other accents), digits and a few marks; the launcher's dialogue boxes draw with it (`PmdText`), and text it cannot draw falls back to Nunito. The boxes' frames follow the game's dialogue boxes and are drawn in code (UI chrome). |
| Display font (titles, buttons, numbers) | `assets/fonts/pokemon_dp.ttf`, `pokemon_dp_bold.ttf` | `Pokemon-DP` and `Pokemon-DP-Bold` from [hexparrot/pokemon-assets](https://github.com/hexparrot/pokemon-assets) (a fork of itsjavi/pokemon-assets, MIT for the package; the fonts are recreations of the games' fonts). Licence file next to them. |
| Backgrounds | `assets/backgrounds/bw01, bw07, bw08, bw11, bw12, bw15.png` | The Pokémon Black/White PC box wallpapers (01, 07, 08, 11, 12, 15), from [kwsch/PKHeX](https://github.com/kwsch/PKHeX) `PKHeX.Drawing.Misc/Resources/img/box/bw` at commit `bfe89c7`. PKHeX is GPL-3 (licence next to them); the art is Game Freak's. Private use only. Tiled, mirrored and drawn at a whole-number scale. |

The same package also has GBC, GBA and DS fonts, menu sprites, ribbons, marks, type badges (SVG), a few more sound effects (collide, doors) and music, if we want more.
